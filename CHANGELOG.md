# What's new in Tile Tunes

## Version 1.1

**▶ Play: https://tejas28599-ship-it.github.io/tile-tunes/**

### Easier to start
- Just tap **Start** and play. The song is already chosen for you; tap **Change** if you'd like a different one.
- The Start tile gently pulses and says "Tap to play", so you always know where to begin.

### Better gameplay
- Tiles never stack in the same lane any more, so two tiles can't look like one long tile.
- Hold tiles now follow the music: they appear on the song's long notes, last as long as the note, and lead straight into the next note with no awkward pause.
- Each tile plays its own note of the song, and a fully held note rings out smoothly into the next one.

### More fun
- A ring and a little ♪ burst from your finger on every correct tap.
- Gentle vibration on taps, hold tiles and game over (on phones that support it).
- New looks as you score: **Sunrise** at 25, **Ocean** at 50, **Forest** at 100, **Twilight** at 150 and **Golden** at 200, each with a confetti burst.
- "Song complete! 🎵" when you play a whole song through.

### Goals and sharing
- Earn up to 3 stars per game: 25, 50 and 100 points.
- Each song shows your best stars and best score.
- **Share score** on the game-over screen sends your score to friends with a link to play.

### More songs
- New: **Jingle Bells**, **Happy Birthday** and **Canon in D**, alongside Ode to Joy, Für Elise and Twinkle Twinkle Little Star.

### Play anywhere, even offline
- After your first visit, Tile Tunes works without internet.
- Add it to your home screen for an app icon that opens full-screen.

## Version 1.0

- The first release: tap the black tiles to play a song, with hold tiles, a speed that rises with your score, piano sounds, and your best score saved.
